# Taz's Angels: Origins, Founder Background, and Business Model (2010s to 2026)

**Method note for the report writer.** Outbound page fetching was blocked in this research session (WebFetch failed to resolve miaminewtimes.com, xxlmag.com, sportskeeda.com). Every finding below therefore comes from search-engine result snippets and summaries of the cited pages, not from a full read of each article. Exact quotes and dates should be re-checked against the original pages before they go into a script or press material. No court docket, corporate filing (Sunbiz), or Wayback Machine snapshot was opened directly.

**Labels used:**
- **VERIFIED**: credible outlet, court record, on-record interview, or the group's or Taz's own official channel (for self-descriptions only).
- **REPORTED**: a single blog, outlet, fan site or aggregator.
- **RUMOR/ALLEGATION**: forum posts, anonymous accounts, gossip with no sourcing.

## 1. Who is Michael "Taz" Williams? (aliases, background, hometown, career, businesses)

### Takeaway
Before Taz's Angels, Taz was a Tampa Bay area music executive and producer. He founded Dirty Down Records and is credited as producer and executive producer on Khia's 2001-02 hit "My Neck, My Back (Lick It)" and her album *Thug Misses*. He now markets himself as the founder of Taz's Angels and the creator of "PolyFly", a relationship philosophy. Sources do not give his age or birth year.

### Cited Findings
- **Legal name and nickname.** He appears as Michael "Taz" Williams in the Miami New Times and in music credits. **VERIFIED** — [Miami New Times](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/news/a-former-tazs-angels-model-has-photo-hacked-sues-to-find-culprit-6560610/); [Wikipedia: My Neck, My Back](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Neck,_My_Back_(Lick_It))
- **Other names.**
  - In an Instagram Live Q&A, Cat and AB call him "The Chosen One." **REPORTED** (group's own framing) — [YouTube: What Poly Fly Life Is Like](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VPGqigYLqQQ)
  - His Facebook page is titled "Taz Williams (@TazsAngels)." **REPORTED** — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/TazsAngels/)
  - A fan-fiction title uses "The Chosen Ones" for the group itself. **REPORTED, fan-made** — [Wattpad](https://www.wattpad.com/85670912-tazsangels-the-chosen-ones-it's-not-just-taz-you)
- **Music career before the group.**
  - "My Neck, My Back (Lick It)" was produced by Michael "Taz" Williams and the Plat'num House team. **VERIFIED** (credits) — [WhoSampled](https://www.whosampled.com/Khia/My-Neck,-My-Back-(Lick-It)/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Neck,_My_Back_(Lick_It))
  - *Thug Misses* first came out on Divine Records on Oct 30, 2001. A wider release followed on Apr 23, 2002, through Dirty Down Records and Artemis Records. Williams is credited as executive producer on at least the European CD. **VERIFIED** — [Wikipedia: Thug Misses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thug_Misses); [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/master/134872-Khia-Thug-Misses)
  - Grokipedia says he was executive producer and A&R. **REPORTED** (low-reliability aggregator) — [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/My_Neck,_My_Back_(Lick_It))
  - RapReviews says Taz and Plat'num House handled the music for the whole album. **REPORTED** — [RapReviews](https://www.rapreviews.com/2002/07/khia-thug-misses/)
- **Falling-out with Khia.** After Khia left Dirty Down, she told MTV that her old label was "the ones that's been spreading the rumors." This is her side only; no reply from the label was found. **VERIFIED** (on the record, one-sided) — [MTV News](https://www.mtv.com/news/1455425/khia-shocked-by-response-to-my-neck-my-back/)
- **Self-description.** His official PolyFly site calls him an "Entrepreneur, founder of Dirty Down Records, founder of Taz's Angels, and creator of PolyFly." It describes a PolyFly "curriculum" taught by "lead instructors Cat and AB, guided by Taz Williams." **VERIFIED** (as his own claim) — [The PolyFly Experience](https://www.thepolyflyexperience.com/)
- **Company and hometown area.**
  - The Miami New Times reported that his company, Gigolo Enterprises LLC, an entertainment company, was registered to his parents' home in Lutz, Florida (Tampa area). The street address is deliberately left out here. **VERIFIED** (credible outlet citing a corporate registration) — [Miami New Times](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/news/former-tazs-angels-model-whose-iphone-was-hacked-sues-to-find-culprit-6394745/)
  - One source says the company opened in 2010 and calls it "a small, fairly new consulting company in Lutz, Florida." **REPORTED** (from a search summary; outlet not identified)
- **His background was opaque even to the press.** The Miami New Times wrote that "their leader's background is mysterious." **VERIFIED** (outlet characterization) — [Miami New Times](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/news/a-former-tazs-angels-model-has-photo-hacked-sues-to-find-culprit-6560610/)
- **Described as a music producer.** A fan wiki calls him "a music producer and is in charge of the Angels hence the name Taz's Angels." **REPORTED** (fan site) — [lovetazsangel fan site](https://lovetazsangel.wixsite.com/tazsangels/tazandothers)

### Inferences
- A "Taz" who produced and executive-produced Khia, a Tampa-area act, and a Gigolo Enterprises registered in Lutz both point to the same Tampa Bay origin. That fits the fan-site claim that Cat was recruited in Tampa (see Section 2). His move into Miami nightlife probably came after his music-label years (2001-2010).
- He was in the music business by about 2001, so he was very likely in his 40s during the group's 2013-2016 peak. No source confirms this.

### Gaps
- His age and birth year were not found in any reliable source.
- No VladTV, XXL or podcast sit-down interview with Taz himself was found. Coverage features Cat and AB speaking for the group.
- There is no confirmed link between Gigolo Enterprises and "Gigolo Records." Sunbiz filings (officers, dates, status) were not checked directly.
- Nothing was found on a criminal record, arrests or charges. No source found reports any charges.
- His other ventures between Khia (2002) and the group (around 2011) are undocumented.

## 3. When and how was Taz's Angels founded? (year, name origin, accounts, follower counts)

### Takeaway
Most secondary sources give **2011** as the founding year. The earliest dated web traces found are from **2013**: a December 2013 blog feature on "@tazsangels_" and a 2013 IMDb-listed video. The name comes from Taz plus *Charlie's Angels*. Follower figures: about 500K (Miami New Times, around 2014), 722K across accounts (2014), and the first account to pass 1M in **September 2015**.

## 4. How did the group make money?

### Takeaway
The only income stream a credible outlet confirms is a **weekly hosting gig at LIV nightclub** (Fontainebleau, Miami Beach). Beyond that, the group is linked to paid party hosting and travel "Concept" trips in other cities, an apparel line (**Caviar Blaque**), a branding and events banner (**"Bad B*tches Link Up"**), and, more recently, PolyFly courses. Escort or prostitution income is alleged widely but has never been proven or charged.

Their videos showed Taz housing about five women in a **$4.5 million South Beach mansion near Alton Road**. Public content was a constant stream of partying, twerking, travel and group photos, framed as a "sisterhood" or "sister wives" household. Critics alleged the group stayed in borrowed or empty mansions, and that the women had to hand their social media over to the group and limit contact with family.

Recruiting reportedly started on Instagram, with Angels liking and DMing a target's photos, and Cat ran the screening.
