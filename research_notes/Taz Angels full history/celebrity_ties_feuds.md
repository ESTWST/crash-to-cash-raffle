## 1. Drake vs. Taz nightclub argument (Miami, 2014) and Drake's "What happened to Taz's Angels" line

### Takeaway
On or just before September 4, 2014, Drake and Taz's Angels (Taz and his entourage) had a loud verbal confrontation, without violence, at Cameo nightclub in Miami Beach during a PartyNextDoor event. It began after Angel "Cat" said on the mic that Drake was "not messing with Taz's Angels." Almost 12 years later, Drake asked "what happened to Taz's Angels?" on "Make Them Cry," the opening track of his ninth album *Iceman* (released May 15, 2026). It is a lyric, not a tweet or interview remark. A separate, unconfirmed fan claim says Drake also referenced Taz and "Angels" on "Star67" (2015).

## 2. The Draya Michele feud

### Takeaway
The best-documented clash dates to **early January 2016** (HelloBeautiful, about January 4, 2016). Angel **Kinky** announced she was leaving the group "after eight years." Draya posted what seemed to be encouraging words, which the Angels took as Draya calling Kinky a "hoe." Angel **Cat** then went after Draya online and in a video. Outlets described it as part of a longer history of pettiness, and a 2012 tweet from the Angels' account confirms they were taunting Draya years earlier.

### Cited Findings
- HelloBeautiful, "Taz Angel 'Cat' Raged Social Media War Against Draya Michele" (URL slug "draya-comes-for-taz-angels"). Syndicated copies on Radio One/Urban One sister sites date it January 4–5, 2016. **REPORTED** (one outlet syndicated across sister sites; the Angels' posts themselves would be primary evidence) — [HelloBeautiful](https://hellobeautiful.com/2837964/draya-comes-for-taz-angels/); [Hot 100.9 syndication](https://hot1009.com/2444531/taz-angel-cat-raged-social-media-war-against-draya-michele/); [105.3 RnB syndication](https://1053rnb.com/3169271/taz-angel-cat-raged-social-media-war-against-draya-michele/)
- Trigger: Kinky told fans she was leaving the crew "after eight years of devoted partying." Draya "seemingly encouraged the woman on her path, but it wasn't taken very well," and "what started as encouraging words…quickly turned into a full out petty social media war." **REPORTED** — [HelloBeautiful](https://hellobeautiful.com/2837964/draya-comes-for-taz-angels/)
- What was said: Cat defended Kinky, saying Draya "insinuated Kinky was a hoe," and added "My number is still the same." In a follow-up video, Cat said she respects any woman's hustle but objected to calling someone out for their grind, and argued that Draya "started from the bottom" herself, so the comment was hypocritical. **REPORTED** (based on Cat's own posts as described by the outlet) — [HelloBeautiful](https://hellobeautiful.com/2837964/draya-comes-for-taz-angels/); [Hot 100.9](https://hot1009.com/2444531/taz-angel-cat-raged-social-media-war-against-draya-michele/)
- Coverage described a "long history of pettiness between these two" and said the tension had been "brewing for years." **REPORTED** — [Hot 100.9](https://hot1009.com/2444531/taz-angel-cat-raged-social-media-war-against-draya-michele/)
- Earlier evidence: on about **October 1, 2012** (decoded from the tweet ID), the official @TazsAngels X/Twitter account, signed "~Cat," replied to a fan who said Cat looked like Draya with a sexual joke tagging @DrayaFace ("Im tryin to put out a sex tape wit her but she fronting!"). **VERIFIED** (primary post from the group's own account) — [X @TazsAngels](https://x.com/tazsangels/status/252866657410752512?lang=en)
- EURweb (March 2016) wrote that "earlier this year, reality star Draya Michele had a brief online beef with Kinky, one of the Taz Angel's, who accused Draya of hoe shaming." This matches the January 2016 episode. **REPORTED** — [EURweb](http://www.eurweb.com/2016/03/taz-angels-slam-critics-sister-wives-lifestyle/)
- Conflicting account: HelloBeautiful's headline has *Cat* leading the attack, while EURweb has *Kinky* making the "hoe shaming" accusation. Both Angels were probably involved; the outlets just name different lead voices.
- Draya's side: no statement from Draya about the exchange turned up. One search summary mentioned a 2017 interview in which Draya said she didn't know Kinky had beef with her, but I could not tie that claim to a specific URL. **Unconfirmed; treat as a gap.**

### Inferences
- The feud seems to be about status and respectability ("hoe-shaming" versus "respect the hustle"). Draya was a reality-TV star (Basketball Wives LA) who had moved into business, and the Angels were Instagram nightlife personalities. Cat's "started from the bottom" jab fits that framing.

### Gaps
- Draya's exact original comment was not retrieved.
- Whether the feud ever resolved is unknown.
- The 2017 Draya interview claim lacks a source.

## 5. How mainstream and hip-hop media portrayed the group over time

Throughout, credible mainstream outlets (Miami New Times, Newsweek, SI, HipHopDX) mention the group only in passing, as context for other news.
